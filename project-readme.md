# BuyNothing.lol

A minimalist web experiment where people can pay $10… to receive absolutely nothing.

## 🧠 Concept

This site exists to prove a point:  
**Marketing > Product.**

There’s no utility, no value, no secret reward.  
Just a big white button that says "Buy Nothing – $10" and a thank-you page that confirms you gave money away voluntarily.

It’s not a scam. It’s not satire. It’s just real.

## 🎯 Goal

Make $1,000+ selling absolutely nothing online, using UGC, TikTok virality, and word-of-mouth.

## 💡 Features

- Stripe Checkout integration
- Unique session gating (only those who pay can see the thank-you page)
- Confetti celebration
- Printable “Certificate of Nothing”
- Terms & Policies to stay legally safe

## 🚫 What It’s Not

- No refunds  
- No random prizes  
- No NFTs  
- No fake scarcity  
- No astrology, fortune-telling, or spiritual bullshit  
- Just clean, intentional, digital absurdity.

## 🔐 Legal

See `/terms` for full terms, refund policy, and privacy statement.  
This is a novelty project and not intended to deceive.

## 🚀 Stack

- Next.js (App Router)
- Stripe Checkout
- Tailwind CSS
